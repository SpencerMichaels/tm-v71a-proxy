1

Before you go

  1. Board ready: AVR in its socket, notch to the left. JP1 shunt parked on one pin.The forward-only firmware is already flashed and passed the bench log check (crystal and lights input both OK).
  2. FT232: disconnect its TXD wire and the 2.2 kΩ from anything going to the board.An idle-high TXD back-powers the AVR through its pin whenever the board's power is off.
  3. Pack: board, meter, logic analyzer, laptop with PulseView, Dupont jumpers, FT232, clipped resistor leads (handy probe pins).Also bring the JP1 shunt (on the board) and the ESD wrist strap, for the bypass fallback in section 5.
2

Body cable only: safety check

This proves the body cable puts 10 V only where it belongs before the head is connected. Nothing goes into J2 yet.

  1. Plug the body cable into J1 (BODY). Head cable stays out. Radio DC on.PB is live whenever the radio has DC, even with no head connected.
  2. Measure each J2 pin to GND (TP2) at its solder joint on the underside.
    Expected on J2
    Pin 1 (PB, square pad)≈ 10 V
    Pin 2 (GND)0 V
    Pin 3 (data line, body → head)≈ 5 V
    Pin 4 (data line, head → body)≈ 5 V
    AVR legs 20 to 21 (top row, 9th and 8th from left)5.0 V
    STOPIf 10 V appears on pin 2, 3 or 4, unplug the body cable and don't connect the head. The cable is probably reversed (1→4). Check it pin for pin before anything else.
    Use the underside diagram for J2: everything is mirrored, so J2 is on the right of J1 from the top but on the left from underneath. Find J2's square pad (pin 1) first. AVR legs 20/21 are measured from the top (second diagram).
3

Head in: forward-only check

  1. Plug the head cable into J2 (HEAD). Turn the radio on at the head.
  2. Use the radio for a few minutes: power on, display, keys, change brightness in the menu, tune around.
    ExpectedEverything works exactly as it did without the board. Every byte from the body to the head is passing through the AVR now.
    STOPIf the display freezes, garbles or stays blank: go to section 5 (bypass). Don't spend long poking at it first.
4

Protocol capture

Same setup as section 3, radio running. This capture sets the constants for the full firmware.

  1. Connect the logic analyzer.
    AnalyzerBoard pointSignal
    GNDTP2 or J4 pin 1Ground
    CH0R6 right lead (x 13.16)Body → head
    CH1R7 right lead (x 26.16)Head → body
    Both right leads feed AVR inputs through 10 kΩ, so they carry the radio's own traffic unchanged, with the AVR in or out.
  2. PulseView: sample at 1–2 MHz. Add a UART decoder on each channel: 57,600 baud, 8N1, idle high, hex output.
    Framing errors? The baud rate is wrong. Measure the narrowest bit with the cursors: 17.4 µs means 57,600.
  3. Record, in one capture: radio power-on; several key presses; stepping brightness through every level in the menu; a minute of idle.
    Look forBrightness messages 33 3x 0D on CH0, where 3x is 30–38 (ASCII '0'–'8').
  4. Optional but useful: with the J3 plug's ILL+ on a 12 V source (parking lights or a 9 V battery), repeat the brightness stepping once.The forward-only firmware ignores ILL, but it shows whether the radio itself sends anything different at night.
  5. Save the capture (.sr) and hand it to Claude.Claude confirms the protocol constants before the full firmware goes in.
  6. Optional, at the same time: FT232 logger. RXD to J4 pin 3, GND to J4 pin 1, nothing on TXD. 
    cd hardware/kenwood_proxy/firmware
make log LOGPORT=/dev/cu.usbserial-A50285BI
    Expect KP v1 rw=0 … and X 1 after power-on. Any O line (head queue overflow) is worth reporting.
5

Bypass: only if section 3 fails

Skip this section if the radio worked in section 3. It tells a firmware problem apart from a board or cable problem.

  1. Radio off. Wrist strap on. Pull the AVR out of its socket.Lever it out evenly from both ends so the legs don't bend.
  2. Fit the JP1 shunt across both pins.
    ⚠️ JP1 rule. Only with the AVR out. With the AVR running, the shunt makes it fight the body's data line.
  3. Radio on.
    Works normallyThe board and cables are fine and the firmware is the problem. Do the capture (section 4) in this state and give it to Claude with a description of what went wrong.
    Still failsThe problem is the jacks or cables. Check the cables pin for pin (a reversed coupler is the usual suspect) and the J1/J2 joints.
  4. Before leaving: radio off, remove the JP1 shunt (park it on one pin), refit the AVR notch left.
6

Full firmware: after Claude reviews the capture

Only once Claude has confirmed the protocol constants. It can be done on the same trip if the review is quick, or on a later one. The radio powers the board; the FT232 never does.

  1. Radio off. Programmer: FT232 TXD → row A, 2.2 kΩ row A → row B, RXD → row B, row B → J4 pin 2, GND → J4 pin 1. 5V/VCC not connected.
    ⚠️ GND on GND. Check the jumper is on the FT232's GND pin, not DTR or CTS next to it.
  2. Radio on. Flash the full firmware. 
    make flash PORT=/dev/cu.usbserial-A50285BI
    The fuses are already set; no need to repeat make fuses.
  3. Radio off. Move the J4 pin 2 jumper to J4 pin 3 and pull the FT232's TXD wire. Start the log, then radio on. 
    make log LOGPORT=/dev/cu.usbserial-A50285BI
  4. Verify: set brightness by day, switch the parking lights on (ILL+ to TAIL, or a 12 V source on J3), set it by night, switch them off, cycle radio power. Watch the log.
    Log lineMeaning
    KP v1 rw=1 d=6 n=2 b=6 nStartup: rewrite on, day slot 6, night slot 2, body's last level 6, lights on (n)
    X 1 / X 0Crystal running / not running
    M n / M dLights changed to night / day
    L n 3Learned level 3 for night
    R 6 2Body sent 6, head got 2
    I n 2Inserted level 2 for night
    WEEPROM written
    OHead queue overflow (should never appear)
    ExpectedThe display switches between your day and night brightness when the lights change, and remembers both after a power cycle.
7

Wrap up

  1. Radio off. Unplug the head and body cables (or leave the board connected if the radio worked and you're happy to run it).The radio can run with the forward-only firmware in place; it behaves exactly like the stock connection.
  2. Give Claude: the .sr capture, the J2 readings if anything looked off, and any log output.
  3. Still to do on the bench: glue Y1 (24 h cure), then close the case only after the full firmware is in. Main guide section 0 has the rest.
